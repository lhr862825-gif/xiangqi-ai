Xiangqi · Chinese Chess

Red to move

How to play

Red moves first, then players alternate turns. Select one of your pieces and click a highlighted point to move or capture.

How the pieces move

  • Chariot / Rook (車): Moves any distance horizontally or vertically, without jumping over pieces.
  • Horse (馬): Moves one point horizontally or vertically, then one point diagonally outward. It is blocked if the first point is occupied.
  • Elephant (相 / 象): Moves exactly two points diagonally. It cannot cross the river or jump over a piece at the midpoint.
  • Advisor (仕 / 士): Moves one point diagonally and must stay inside the palace (the marked 3 × 3 area).
  • General (帥 / 將): Moves one point horizontally or vertically inside the palace. The two generals cannot face each other on the same file with no piece between them.
  • Cannon (炮): Moves like a rook without capturing. To capture, it must jump over exactly one intervening piece.
  • Soldier (兵 / 卒): Moves one point forward. After crossing the river, it may also move one point sideways. It never moves backward.

How to win

An attacked general is in check and must be protected. You cannot make a move that leaves your general in check. A player loses if they have no legal moves, whether in check (checkmate) or not (stalemate).

Modes and controls

In Two players mode, both sides share this board. Against the AI, you play Red and the AI plays Black; choose a difficulty to suit you. Undo takes back one move in Two players mode, or returns to your turn against the AI. New game resets the board.

Keyboard: Use arrow keys to move the cursor, and Enter or Space to select and move. Press Esc or the × button to close these instructions.
